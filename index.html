TAFIA

Medication + Engagement

Stay on top of meds, without nagging.

TAFIA is a gentle companion for older adults that:
• tracks medications,
• adds light brain games,
• and gives caregivers peace of mind with simple updates.

• Older adults who want a calm, simple routine.

• Families who want one place to see “how today went.”
